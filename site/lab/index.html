Five ways to play

Same cast, same actions, much less reading. Play each one and see which loop you'd want to keep playing.

  1. 1
    EvidenceOne evidence card, no article. Draw a graph that explains it. Blocks you get wrong will tell you.
  2. 2
    WiggleA secret graph. Tap a block to wiggle it and see what wiggles back. Fewer wiggles, more stars.
  3. 3
    ParWiggle as much as you like, but every hole has a par: the fewest arrows that explain everything.
  4. 4
    Rapid fireHeadlines flash up. Six seconds to pick the shape: A causes B, B causes A, or a sneaky third block.
  5. 5
    CastNo text, just twists. Mr Confounder sneaks onto the board. The Bouncer shuts the door.
