Try your own data

Upload a CSV, choose your columns, and draw what you think causes what. Your file never leaves your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

2 Check your columns

We've guessed how to use each column. Change anything that looks wrong. Numbers and yes / no columns can be causes or effects. Categories without an order, like region, can only be adjusted for.

How to draw

Drag a block to move it. Drag from its round handle onto another block to draw an arrow. Click an arrow to reverse or remove it. Leave a column off the canvas if you think it causes neither your cause nor your effect.

Effect of on

Choose a cause and an effect above. They appear on the canvas, and you can add the other columns you think matter.
