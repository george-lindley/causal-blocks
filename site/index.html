Do small towns really educate children better?

The ONS found that children in England's smaller towns do better at school. That is a correlation. Draw the causes and the story changes: the gap is about deprivation, and among towns equally deprived, larger ones come out ahead. Every estimate below is DoWhy run on 1,082 English towns.
