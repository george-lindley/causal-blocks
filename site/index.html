Meet the family

You already know them: they're in your life too.

  • SuspectAlways pushing. Everyone says she makes things happen. Does she really?
  • EffectEasily surprised. Things keep happening to him, and everyone wants to know why.
  • Mr ConfounderThe puppet master. Pulls two strings at once, so two things rise together without touching.
  • Ms MediatorAlways in a hurry, carrying the message from one to the other. Stop her and the effect stops.
  • Mr BouncerDecides who gets in. Look only at who's inside, and strangers seem linked.
  • The BystanderJust passing by. Minds his own business, so he can't fake a link.
  • Ms NudgeThe naughty friend with a slingshot. Never does it herself: she eggs our Suspect on.
  • Baby WobbleCan't sit still. Jiggles the Effect about, so it's hard to read.

What you didn't learn at school

Causal Blocks teaches the difference between correlation and causation by having you draw it: causal diagrams, confounders, mediators, colliders, and why a fair comparison means controlling for the right things. Made for anyone 12 and up, from first-timers to grown-ups who never got taught it: the later levels use real research data.
